Financial Formulas

Return on Investment (ROI):

\[ ROI = \frac{Net\ Profit}{Investment\ Cost} \times 100\% \]

Compound Annual Growth Rate (CAGR):

\[ CAGR = \left(\frac{FV}{PV}\right)^{\frac{1}{n}} - 1 \]

Remember to explain these formulas clearly. ROI measures profitability. CAGR shows average annual growth over time.
